🏆 Behind the Scenes: HackTheAI – Intern University Hackathon (Bangladesh)

Organized by Green University Computer Club (GUCC)

Presented by Md. Showaib Rahman Tanveer • Abdul Fattah • Nurul Huda


🟩 Introduction

HackTheAI – Intern University Hackathon (Bangladesh) was an exciting national-level hackathon organized to explore the potential of Generative AI among university students.
The event brought together innovators, problem solvers, and creative minds to build meaningful AI-powered projects.

This webpage presents the complete story behind the event — how it started, how it was executed, and what we learned along the way.

Organizing Team:
Green University Computer Club (GUCC)
in collaboration with SmythOS, GDG Sonargaon, and Intern University.


🚀 About the Hackathon

HackTheAI is a global initiative designed to empower students to learn, build, and experiment with Generative AI technologies.
The Bangladesh round was hosted by the Green University Computer Club (GUCC) — a proud chapter that encourages innovation, learning, and collaboration among students.

Core Objectives:

  • Encourage students to apply AI in real-world problem-solving
  • Provide mentorship and exposure to AI technologies
  • Create collaboration between universities, mentors, and global AI platforms

Theme: Generative AI for Real-World Impact

Event Type: Virtual Hackathon
Duration: Multi-day event including registration, mentoring, and final presentation


💡 How It All Started

The journey began when GUCC was invited to collaborate as the organizing partner for the Bangladesh round of the HackTheAI Intern University Hackathon.
The idea immediately resonated with the team — an opportunity to engage students in cutting-edge AI innovation.

We began with:

  • Initial meetings with the main organizers (SmythOS, GDG Sonargaon)
  • Setting up goals and expectations
  • Building a plan that suited both the international framework and our local participants

From day one, our motto was clear:

“Plan smart, communicate clearly, execute smoothly.”


🧩 Planning & Coordination

The entire operation was strategically divided into multiple stages:

Stage 1: Core Team Formation

  • Tanveer (President, GUCC): Overall coordination, communication with main organizers, content development, announcements, and updates
  • Abdul Fattah: Technical coordination, form creation, data management, and participant support
  • Nurul Huda: Volunteer management, scheduling, and logistics handling

Stage 2: Planning Sessions
We conducted online and offline discussions to finalize:

  • Registration process
  • Timeline and deliverables
  • Volunteer assignments
  • Mentorship plan
  • Final submission and judging process

Stage 3: Tool Setup
We relied on multiple tools for seamless workflow:

  • Google Forms & Sheets – Registration and participant tracking
  • Google Drive – File sharing and submission storage
  • Messenger & WhatsApp – Internal communication
  • Canva – Design and marketing content
  • Gmail & Notion – Updates, document sharing, and email announcements


🧾 Registration Process

Registration was opened to students nationwide from AI, CS, and IT backgrounds.

Steps:

  1. Registration via Google Form
  2. Confirmation email with event details
  3. Formation of participant groups and mentor allocation
  4. Orientation and briefing session before the main event

Highlights:

  • Overwhelming response from students across multiple universities
  • Balanced mix of beginner and experienced participants
  • Organized data tracking of registrants and teams


🛠️ Pre-Event Activities

Before the hackathon, several preparatory steps ensured readiness:

  • Orientation Session: Explained the hackathon structure, rules, timeline, and expectations
  • Mentor Introduction: Participants met their assigned mentors who guided them during the hackathon
  • Tech Checks: Ensured all participants had proper access to submission portals and meeting platforms
  • Team Briefings: Each organizing member had defined communication clusters for quick updates

These pre-event efforts built clarity and excitement for the main day.


💻 The Hackathon Day

The main event was intense, collaborative, and inspiring.

Event Flow:

  1. Kickoff Session: Introduction from GUCC and SmythOS
  2. Team Work Time: Participants developed their projects under mentor supervision
  3. Mentor Check-ins: Regular syncs to guide progress
  4. Submission Phase: Teams submitted their final solutions
  5. Judging & Review: Judges evaluated based on innovation, feasibility, and impact
  6. Closing Ceremony: Winners announced, feedback shared, and appreciation given

Atmosphere:

  • Continuous collaboration and communication
  • Energetic participation from students
  • Technical discussions, creative problem-solving, and mentor interaction throughout the day


⚡ Challenges We Faced

No event is without obstacles — but each challenge made us stronger and more adaptive.

Key Challenges:

  • Time constraints and tight scheduling
  • Managing real-time communication among multiple groups
  • Technical glitches (submission issues, network interruptions)
  • Handling participant queries and last-minute confusion

How We Overcame Them:

  • Effective division of responsibilities
  • Quick internal messaging for urgent updates
  • Backup communication channels (email + WhatsApp)
  • Maintaining a calm, solution-focused mindset

“The key was not avoiding problems — it was solving them together.”


🌟 Highlights & Achievements

Despite challenges, the hackathon was a resounding success.

Event Highlights:

  • Dozens of creative project submissions using Generative AI

Memorable Moments:
